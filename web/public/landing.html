FAQ

Questions
answered

Do I need to use AI for every message?
No — and you shouldn't. AI works best for the first inquiry from a new customer, where they ask about price, availability, and delivery all at once. Those are the long, repetitive messages that take 2-3 minutes to type. Follow-up messages mid-conversation ("okay, send it"), order confirmations, and simple one-word replies are faster to type yourself.
Does it write in Romanized Nepali?
Yes — that's the core feature. The AI detects how the customer wrote their message and replies in the exact same style. If they wrote in Romanized Nepali, the reply is in Romanized Nepali. Never Devanagari.
Do I need to select which product the customer is asking about?
No. The AI reads the customer message and automatically matches it to the right product from your catalogue. If the message is unclear, it asks a natural clarifying question instead of guessing.
Can I pay with eSewa or Khalti?
Yes — eSewa and Khalti are both supported. You don't need an international card or a bank account. Just your eSewa or Khalti wallet.
What happens when I hit the free limit?
On the free plan you get 20 AI replies per day. When you hit the limit, you'll see an upgrade prompt. Your account and products are never deleted — you just need to wait until the next day or upgrade to Pro for unlimited replies.
Does it automatically send replies to customers?
No — and that's intentional. You always review and send the reply yourself. The AI generates the reply, you copy it and send it on WhatsApp or Instagram. You stay in control.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Cancel anytime from your account settings. No contracts, no questions asked. Your account stays on free plan after cancellation.
